## Prohibited words

<Warning>
  VAS's own FAQ flags this list as **"updated regularly"** and recommends always re-checking via
  the live [compliance endpoint](/business-name/endpoints#name-compliance-check) rather than
  hardcoding. Treat the list below as a snapshot for client-side pre-validation, not a substitute
  for the real-time API call.
</Warning>

wealth · company · empire · investment · finance · force · state · national · federal ·
cooperative · limited · betting · sport · construction · mining · quarry · exploration · mosque ·
church · institute · cash · monie · money · foundation · bank · center/centre · lmt/ltd · inc ·
holding · group · consortium · funds · exchange · moni · incorporated · academy · school · ex ·
fx · apostolic

<Note>
  Notably broader than what might be assumed: generic-sounding words like **"group," "holding,"
  "center/centre," "exchange," and "consortium"** are flatly prohibited, not just flagged for
  review. A BN application like "XYZ Group" or "ABC Trading Center" will fail compliance outright
  — this isn't the same, shorter list of Government/Trust/University-style restricted words that
  applies to CAC's portal generally. Check both; they don't fully overlap.
</Note>

## Qualifier rules

Exactly **one** qualifier, and it must be **trailing**:

- Missing a qualifier entirely → [code `02`](/business-name/compliance-codes),
  `QUALIFIER_NOT_FOUND`. Suggested qualifiers: ventures, concepts, enterprises, beauty, fashion,
  services, hub, biz.
- More than one qualifier (e.g. both "ventures" and "enterprises") → [code `13`](/business-name/compliance-codes),
  `MULTIPLE_QUALIFIERS`.
- A qualifier that exists but isn't at the end of the name → [code `14`](/business-name/compliance-codes),
  `NON_TRAILING_QUALIFIER`.

## Address quality

Good addresses are specific and locatable, not necessarily formal. VAS's own examples:

- `"Plot 29, Icpc Avenue, Ikoyi, Lagos Island, Lagos"` — a formal street address.
- `"Chief Eyo Ita compound Umu Village Ekop Ide community Ado LGA Oyo State"` — a landmark-relative
  description, acceptable when a formal street address doesn't exist.
- `"Opposite Winners Chapel Umu village EkopIde Community Ado LGA Oyo state"` — same pattern.

## Full guidelines to prevent a query

From VAS's own FAQ, in order of impact:

1. **Compliance score ≥75%** — see [Compliance codes](/business-name/compliance-codes#practical-use)
   for why this is a soft signal, not a hard rule.
2. **Proprietor is not a minor and not a foreigner.** Business Name registration is
   [Nigerian-proprietor-only](/business-name/overview) — a hard API constraint, not a soft
   guideline.
3. **Images are clear and appropriate** — avoid anything that could read as explicit content.
4. **Proposed name is more than a single word.**
5. **Addresses are clear and descriptive** — see examples above.
6. **Line of business isn't prohibited** — validate via the compliance endpoint rather than
   assuming.
7. **All required fields pass validation** — see [Pre-Registration Validation](/business-name/endpoints#pre-registration-validation)
   for the full field-level contract (date formats, email format, gender enum values, etc.).
8. **No prohibited words** — see the list above.
